This site and the public repository describe a research prototype / testnet. Nothing here is a live mainnet, a securities offering, or a token sale.

Research notice

ADDITION is experimental software published at https://github.com/ADDAddition/ADDITION. The default network is addition-testnet. An opt-in mainnet profile exists for local experiments and is not a public live chain.

Pages that talk to RPC fail closed: if the node does not answer, they show RPC offline and do not invent blocks, heights, hashrate, node counts, or supply.

No sale, no listing claims

  • No token sale, ICO, or wallet-connect purchase flow on this site.
  • No exchange listing claim.
  • No fabricated liquidity, price, or circulating supply.
  • Kernel constants such as a 50,000,000 cap are design parameters, not a marketed float.
