● Privacy

Privacy model

ADDITION has two separate privacy paths in the code. Here is what each one actually does.

Pre-mainnet. ADDITION mainnet has not launched. The code is public and tested on local and research testnets; there is no live mainnet, no listed coin and no sale.
Shielded pool

STARK proofs

Shielded notes are spent with STARK proofs and nullifiers, and memos are encrypted. Fully shielded transactions relay through the mempool. Tested on local testnets only.

Opening RPCs

Not zero-knowledge

The older privacy_*_open RPCs use a SHA3-512 commitment plus a nullifier opening. The node sees the secret, which is why getinfo reports privacy_claim=opening_not_zk. Do not treat them as private.

Network privacy

Peers connect over Tor v3 onion services, and the mainnet profile is onion-only. The wallet UI on this site talks only to your own node on 127.0.0.1.
